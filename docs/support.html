MilBudgetBuddy Support

MilBudgetBuddy is a personal finance tool built for US military service members, veterans, and military families — pay estimates, budget tracking, PCS/travel calculators, retirement planning, and more.

Need help?
Email us at support@milbudgetbuddy.com for bug reports, feature requests, billing/subscription questions, or account/data deletion requests. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Common Questions

How do I delete my account and data?
In the app: Settings → Delete Account (if signed in), or Legal & Privacy → Data & Account Deletion. This permanently and immediately removes your account and all cloud-synced data.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?
iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → MilBudgetBuddy → Cancel.
Android: Google Play Store → Subscriptions → MilBudgetBuddy → Cancel.

Is my data accurate?
All pay figures are estimates based on public DoD/IRS/VA data, kept current with each release. Always verify your actual pay at myPay.dfas.mil.
