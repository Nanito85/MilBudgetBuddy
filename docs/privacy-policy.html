PRIVACY POLICY

MilBudgetBuddy

Effective date: May 18, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 18, 2026

Short version: MilBudgetBuddy stores all your data locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. The app works fully offline except for the optional AI assistant feature.

1. Who We Are

MilBudgetBuddy ("the app," "we," "us") is a personal finance tool built for U.S. military service members and their families. The app is developed and maintained as an independent project.

Contact: Alonebum@protonmail.com

2. Data We Collect

MilBudgetBuddy does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All information you enter is saved exclusively on your device using Android's local storage (AsyncStorage).

Information stored locally on your device includes:

  • Military branch, pay grade, and years of service (for pay calculations)
  • Last name and nickname (for personalized display only)
  • Duty station ZIP code and state of residence (for BAH and tax estimates)
  • Family size and dependent status (for entitlement calculations)
  • TSP contribution percentage, SGLI opt-out status
  • Monthly budget category amounts
  • Special pay entries
  • Kids' profiles, goals, and chores (stored locally, never transmitted)
  • Saved daily tips and notification preferences
  • One-time disclaimer acknowledgment flag

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

3. AI Assistant (Optional Feature)

The app includes an optional AI financial assistant powered by Anthropic's Claude API. This feature is only active when an API key is configured.

When you use the AI chat feature:

  • Messages you type are sent to Anthropic's servers to generate a response
  • Your service branch and pay grade context may be included to personalize responses
  • Anthropic's privacy policy applies to these interactions: anthropic.com/privacy
  • Chat history is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to us

If you do not use the AI chat feature, no data of any kind leaves your device.

4. Analytics and Tracking

MilBudgetBuddy does not use:

  • Analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • Advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • Crash reporting services that transmit data off-device
  • Social media tracking pixels or SDKs
  • Device fingerprinting or persistent identifiers

5. Permissions

The app may request the following Android permissions:

  • Notifications — to deliver optional daily finance tips at a time you choose. You can disable this at any time in the app or in Android Settings.
  • Internet — required only for the AI chat feature. All calculator and tool features work fully offline.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. There is no personal data on our servers because we do not operate servers that receive personal data.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All app data is stored on your device and is under your full control. To delete all data:

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → MilBudgetBuddy → Storage → Clear Data
  • Or uninstall the app

Either action permanently removes all locally stored data. We have no copy of it to delete on our end.

8. Children's Privacy

MilBudgetBuddy is intended for adults (18+). The app includes a kids' goals and chores section designed to be used by parents on behalf of their children. No children's data is transmitted off-device. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

9. Financial Disclaimer

MilBudgetBuddy provides educational estimates only. All figures — pay calculations, BAH amounts, TRICARE estimates, retirement projections — are approximations for informational purposes and may not reflect your actual entitlements or costs. This app is not a financial advisor. Always verify entitlements with your finance office, MyPay (dfas.mil), or a qualified financial professional.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
