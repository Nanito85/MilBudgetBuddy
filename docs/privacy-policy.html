Privacy Policy

🔐 Your data stays on your device

Last updated: June 2026

MilBudgetBuddy ("the App") is a personal finance tool for US military service members, veterans, and military families. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about how your data is handled.

1. What Data We Collect

  • Local device storage: Your profile (branch, rank, years of service), budget categories, expenses, savings goals, net worth entries, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you sign in to sync.
  • Cloud sync (optional): If you create an account and sign in, your data is synced to a private Firebase Realtime Database. Only you can access this data using your credentials.
  • Account information: If you create an account, we store your email address only. This is used for authentication and account recovery.
  • Anonymous usage analytics: We collect anonymous analytics about which features are used (e.g., "user opened Pay Chart"). No personally identifiable information, pay amounts, or financial data is included in analytics.

2. What We Never Collect

  • Social Security Numbers or government ID numbers
  • Bank account or routing numbers
  • Actual LES data from DFAS or myPay
  • Location data
  • Contact lists, camera, or microphone access
  • Any data from children's profiles (Kids / Cadet HQ stores only a nickname and savings goals)

3. How Your Data Is Protected

  • On-device: All local data is protected by your device's built-in encryption and lock screen security (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore where applicable).
  • In transit: All communications with Firebase are encrypted using TLS 1.2+.
  • At rest (cloud): Firebase encrypts all stored data at rest using AES-256.
  • No sale: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): Used for authentication and optional cloud sync. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo / EAS: Used for app distribution and over-the-air updates. Expo does not have access to your financial data.
  • Sentry: Used for anonymous crash reporting. No financial data or PII is included in crash reports.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: Standard app store analytics apply per their respective policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The Kids / Cadet HQ feature allows parents to create savings profiles for their children. Only a nickname and savings goal amounts are stored. No personally identifiable information is collected from minors. This app is not directed at children under 13. Parents control all child data and can delete it at any time.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app at any time — profile, budget, expenses, goals, and net worth.
  • Export: Use the Financial Readiness Worksheet (Tools → Financial Command) to generate and share a PDF of your financial data.
  • Delete local data: Settings → Reset All Data permanently wipes all data from this device.
  • Delete account and cloud data: Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your account and all synced cloud data.
  • Opt out of analytics: Analytics are anonymous and aggregated. Contact us if you have concerns.

7. Data Retention

Local data is retained on your device until you uninstall the app or use Reset All Data. Cloud data is retained until you delete your account. Anonymous analytics data may be retained for up to 24 months for product improvement purposes.

8. California / CCPA and GDPR

We do not sell personal data as defined under CCPA. EU/EEA users may exercise rights of access, correction, and deletion by contacting us. Our legal basis for processing is your consent (account creation) and legitimate interest (app functionality).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

10. Financial Disclaimer

All pay estimates, calculators, and projections in MilBudgetBuddy are for informational purposes only. They are based on publicly available DoD pay tables and do not constitute financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. Always verify your pay with your official LES from myPay.dfas.mil.
